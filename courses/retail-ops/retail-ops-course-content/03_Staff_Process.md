# Video 3 — The Staff Process

## MODULE 2 — THE STAFF PROCESS

### 2.1 Why this process exists
A well-stocked, well-displayed store still fails if the person standing at the counter can't explain the product or handle a customer. This process governs the full lifecycle of a store employee — called a **CCA (Customer Care Associate)** — from hiring through ongoing performance rating.

### 2.2 Step-by-step walkthrough

**Step 1 — Training Methodology** *(the framework everything else sits inside)*
> 🟡 **Sticky note — "Define":**
> - **Training modules:** Basic Hygiene, Product Training, Sales Training (Storytelling / Upselling / Cross-selling)
> - **Training Format:** Google Meet (online) or in-person Floor Training
> - **Timeline:** Day 0 / 15 / 30 / 45 Evaluation checkpoints

**Step 2 — Induction with HR (HR team)**
Per the transcript, induction happens on a **fixed weekly cadence — every Wednesday** — regardless of when the person was actually hired.

**Step 3 — Connect with Area Manager**
Post-induction, the new hire is formally routed to their **Area Manager or city supervisor** — this is the person who owns their training from here on.

**Step 4 — "3 Types of Training" (the fork)**
This single box is the gateway into three parallel training tracks, run one after another: **Basic Hygiene → Product Training → Sales Training.**

#### Track A: Basic Hygiene Training
- **Conducted by:** Supervisor / AM
- **Mode:** PDF Guidelines *(same PDF for every new hire — "it is a general PDF for everyone")*
- **Includes:** Grooming, Uniform, Cleanliness Standard
- **Decision — "Hygiene Standard Understood?"**
  - **No** → loop back for re-training on the same module
  - **Yes** → proceed onward to the next test/tracking stage

#### Track B: Product Training
- **Conducted by:** Supervisor / AM
- **Mode:** Online meet + **Product Knowledge Session**
- **Includes:** Plant usage, Care instructions
- Then: **Floor Training (Practical)**, conducted by the **Store Supervisor**, over a defined **Duration of 8–10 days**
  - **Includes:** Customer handling, Product placement
- **Decision — "Training Completed Successfully?"**
  - **No** → recycle through more floor training
  - **Yes** → **Conduct Test**

**The Product Knowledge Test** covers **30–35 questions** related to the product range, run by HR (this detail comes from a linked note further along the flow).

#### Track C: Sales Training
This is the most conceptually rich track — it teaches the CCA *how to sell*, not just what to say. The chart defines all three techniques explicitly:

> 🟡 **Define Storytelling:** Explaining the plant in a lifestyle + emotional + benefit-driven way.
> *Example: "This is a Snake Plant — it's perfect for bedrooms because it releases oxygen at night and needs very little care. Even if you forget watering, it survives easily."*

> 🟡 **Define Upselling:** Encouraging a better/premium version of the plant or setup.
> *Example: "This one comes in a basic pot, but this ceramic self-watering planter will keep the plant healthy for longer and also enhance your room aesthetics."*

> 🟡 **Define Cross-selling:** Suggesting related products (fertilizer, watering can, decorative pots, plant stand).
> *Example: "Along with this plant, you can take this organic fertilizer — it will help it grow faster."*

- **Conducted by:** AM / AGM
- **Decision — "Training Completed Successfully?"**
  - **No** → recycle
  - **Yes** → **Conduct Test** → **Sales Pitch Test by HR**

The **Sales Pitch Test** is a live role-play: the CCA acts as staff, the evaluator acts as a customer, and the CCA's real-time handling is scored — described in the meeting as *"a five-minute test kind of a thing."*

**Step 5 — Performance Tracking** *(where all three tracks converge)*
> 🟡 **Sticky note — "Timelines / Checkpoints":** Day 0 (Joining), Day 15, Day 30, Day 45

**Step 6 — Conduct Test → Rating Parameters**
> 🟡 **Sticky note:** Rating parameters = **Product Knowledge Test** + **Sales Pitch Test**

**Step 7 — Give Rating → Check Rating (minimum 7 required)**
> 🟡 **Sticky note — scoring guide:** *"7 is not good, 8 requires continuous improvement, 9 and above is OK."* This means a passing score of 7 is only the bare minimum — the organization's real bar is 8–9+.

**Step 8 — Decision: "Rating >= 7?"**
- **Yes** → **Share feedback with HR** → **Employee confirmation** *(the CCA is formally confirmed in their role)*
- **No** → **Re-training** → **1-on-1 discussion with AM**

**Step 9 — The Re-training Loop (for scores below 7)**
> 🟡 **Sticky note — "Identify gaps basis of training-specific performance":**
> 1. Product Knowledge
> 2. Communication
> 3. Confidence
> 4. Sales Skills
> 5. Hygiene

This feeds into **Create Improvement Plan**:
> 🟡 **Sticky note:** Assign specific areas to improve + Set timelines to improve

→ **Schedule Re-training** → **Decision: "Satisfactory Rating in 45 days?"**
- **No** → **Inform HR for termination** *(this is the process's hard exit point)*
- **Yes** → loops back (green arrow) into **Employee confirmation**

> 💡 **Meeting context worth knowing:** as of this review, the *scoring mechanism itself* (how monthly ratings are objectively generated for every CCA) is still being finalized by HR — described as currently "subjective" with a goal to make it "objective going forward." As a fresher, if you're asked to help formalize this, that's a live, in-progress initiative, not a finished system.

### ✅ Module 2 Quick Recap
- Fixed induction day: **every Wednesday**
- Three training tracks, always in this order: **Hygiene → Product → Sales**
- Floor training duration: **8–10 days**
- Passing rating: **≥7** (but 9+ is the real target)
- Evaluation checkpoints: **Day 0 / 15 / 30 / 45**
- Improvement window before termination risk: **45 days**
