## 0. Orientation — What is "Retail Ops" and how is this map organized?

Retail Ops (Offline Sales) is the team responsible for everything that happens to a product **after** it's made and **before** it reaches a customer's hands inside a physical retail partner store (e.g., a "Home Center" — referred to as **HC** or **NG** throughout this course).

The master diagram splits Retail Ops into **four core processes**, and — importantly — they are meant to be learned in this order, because that's the order the operations team itself walks through in a review:

| # | Process | One-line purpose |
|---|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Stock** | Getting the right quantity of the right product to the right store, on time |
| 2 | **Staff** | Hiring, training, testing, and rating the people who sell the product |
| 3 | **Display** | Making sure the product is arranged correctly, looks saleable, and is properly counted |
| 4 | **Communication** | Making sure signage, offers, and product info are visible, accurate, and understood by the customer |

Every box in every process carries a banner at the top that reads **"Primary Responsibility – AM / Secondary Responsibility – AGM."** Memorize this now — it means the **Area Manager (AM)** owns execution of *everything* you're about to learn, and the **AGM Sales** is the backstop/escalation point. Keep this in your head as you go; we won't repeat it every time.

**Diagram color code**, used consistently across all four modules:
- **White/black boxes** = an action or process step
- **Diamonds** = a decision point (usually Yes/No)
- **Yellow sticky notes** = definitions, formulas, or extra rules attached to the nearest box
- **Green arrows** = the "Yes" / positive path
- **Red arrows** = the "No" / problem path
- **Tan/beige boxes** = a cadence or timing rule (e.g., "Every Saturday," "Weekly")
- **Orange/red box** = a hand-off to another process entirely (e.g., "Retail O2D Process")
