# Video 6 — Communication Process Part 2: Product & Offer

#### 4.2.C — Product Communication
This is the individual **shelf talker** — the small sign/label attached to (or near) a specific product.

> 🟡 **What a product talker includes:** Product name, Category (Indoor / Outdoor / Gifting / Premium), Care requirements (sunlight, water), Price range.

- **Check talkers are placed with the right category**
- **Decision: "Is Communication Correctly mapped to product?"** — verified *"via O2O, plant family level"*
  - **Yes** → **Click Pictures & send to AM** → **Decision: "Placement as per plan?"**
    - **Yes** → **Terminate Process**
    - **No** → **Re-arrange** → **Check by Supervisor** → loops back
  - **No** → **Re-arrange** → **Check by Supervisor** → loops back into the mapping check

> 💡 **Bonus detail from the meeting, not fully captured on the diagram:** for some product families (the meeting cites the Syngonium family as an example), NG has gone a step further and added a **QR code directly on the plant tag**, linking to a YouTube explainer covering care instructions, benefits, and use-case (air-purifying, aesthetic, etc.) — an enhanced version of standard product communication.

#### 4.2.D — Offer Communication
This governs discount/promotional signage — described in the meeting as running through **two layers**: a handful of **"core" offers that run all year** on specific SKUs, plus **seasonal campaigns** like the twice-yearly **EOS (End of Season Sale)**.

- **Offers Should be shown clearly**
- **Decision: "Is offer easy to understand in a single read?"**
  - **Yes** → offer type can be: **Discount / Bundle / Seasonal Sale / Festive Promotion**
    > 🟡 **Example clarity bar on chart:** *"Offer is simple & understandable. Clear CTA (e.g., Buy 2 Get 1, Flat 20% Off). Easy to understand messaging, no confusion."*
  - **No** → **Coordinate with design team: simplify wording, redesign communication**
- Both paths converge into **Check placement**
  > 🟡 **Placement rule:** *"Near product, Entry visibility"*
- **Decision: "Is Product offer Linkage correct?"**
  > 🟡 **Standard:** *"Offer clearly mapped to product. Customer knows 'which product → which offer.'"*
  - This connects back into the shared **verification loop** (Click Pictures & send to AM → Placement as per plan? → Terminate / Re-arrange) used across the Category and Product communication branches.

> 💡 **Why this matters (from the meeting):** offer-communication compliance directly affects a store team's individual **KPI rating** — the meeting recounts a real example where a category's offer talkers were missing at certain outlets, which negatively impacted that team's performance rating. This is explicitly framed as being important for both the **customer experience** (customers actively look for visible discount cues) and the **brand's own performance tracking.**

### ✅ Module 4 Quick Recap
- Four communication types: **Brand, Category/Concept, Product, Offer**
- Brand signage non-compliance SLA ladder: **AM (7 days) → AGM → CEO (15 days)**
- Category/Concept ideas: **minimum 6+ per year**, sourced from NPD & Social teams
- Product talkers must show: **name, category, care needs, price**
- Offer talkers must pass the **"single read" clarity test** and be placed **near the product with entry visibility**
- Nearly every branch ends the same way: **photograph → verify against plan → terminate (if correct) or re-arrange and re-check (if not)**
