# Video 5 — Communication Process Part 1: Brand & Category/Concept

## MODULE 4 — THE COMMUNICATION PROCESS

### 4.1 Why this process exists
"Communication" here doesn't mean internal messaging — it means **every piece of in-store messaging the customer sees**: brand signage, category concepts, individual product info tags, and promotional offers. This process ensures all of it is present, correctly placed, and easy to understand.

### 4.2 Step-by-step walkthrough

**Step 1 — Communication Planning → Decision: "Planogram (VM guidelines) available?"**
- **No** → **Check if planogram has talker/dangler guidance** *(a "talker" is a small shelf sign; a "dangler" hangs from above)* → **Arrange from NPD/Design team**
- **Yes** → **Supervisor Conduct Brief Session about Communication type**
  > 🟡 **Session Includes:** Brand Knowledge, Concept Knowledge, Product Knowledge, Offers Knowledge

**Step 2 — "Types of Communications"**
This is the fork into the **four communication categories** covered for the rest of this module. The chart also lists the product concept buckets these apply across: **Table Top Plants, Floor Plants, Flowering Plants, Gifting Range, Planters, Stands, Plant Care.**

---

#### 4.2.A — Brand Communication
> 🟡 **Definition on chart:** *"All brand visual and messaging elements (logo, banners, signage, offers) used in-store to inform, attract, and influence customer's brand recall."*

- **Decision: "NG Branding visible?"**
  - **No** → **Escalate to AM**, with a **7-day resolution SLA**
  - **Yes** → **Decision: "NG Brand signage present?"**
    - **No** → **Escalate to AM** (same 7-day SLA path)
    - **Yes** → **Check: Signage present + if LED, is it powered on** → **Decision: "Is visible?"**
      - **No** → **Check: Entry visibility + Clear line of sight** — this doubles as a self-correcting loop back into the visibility check once fixed
      - **Yes** → **Terminate process** *(this branch is compliant — nothing further needed)*

**If escalated to AM (any "No" above):**
→ **Check placement as per planogram** → **Activation with Store Supervisor** *(this is where the meeting clarifies: NG only supplies the signage design/dimensions — the actual physical mounting of the backlit panel is managed by the retail store's own team, not NG staff directly)*
→ If the 7-day SLA lapses unresolved: **AM escalates to AGM**, and if still unresolved, **escalate to CEO within a 15-day SLA.**

---

#### 4.2.B — Category / Concept Communication
This covers thematic storytelling at a category level — e.g., positioning a group of plants around an "air-purifying" concept rather than promoting one SKU.

- **Category Name** must be simple and clear
  > 🟡 **Example on chart:** *"Easy to Understand — For Eg: Low-Maintenance Plants"*
- **Annual Idea Target:** a **minimum of 6+ concept ideas per year**, sourced from the **NPD and Social media teams**
- **Decision: "Check monthly implementation"**
  - **No** → **Escalate to AM** → **Align with NPD/Social Team** → **Received** (loops back into the placement check)
  - **Yes** → **Decision: "Placed according to VM guidelines?"**
    - **Yes** → **Check by Supervisor** → **Click Pictures & send to AM** → **Decision: "Placement as per plan?"**
      - **Yes** → **Terminate Process**
      - **No** → **Re-arrange** → **Check by Supervisor** → loops back to re-photograph
